文法與易混淆重點

介系詞後接 V-ing

老師特別提醒：介系詞後若接動作，通常使用 V-ing。例：the advantage of studying / the advantage to studying。

研究／數據＋顯示

research / study / report / evidence 後常接 suggest, show, indicate, demonstrate 等，表示「研究／資料顯示」。

關聯性表達

be linked to / be related to / be associated with / be connected with。注意介系詞搭配不同。

effect / affect / effort

effect 多作名詞「效果／影響」；affect 多作動詞「影響」；effort 是「努力」。

effective / efficient

effective = 有效、重結果；efficient = 有效率、重過程與資源使用；efficiency 是名詞「效率」。

answer to the question

「問題的答案」用 the answer to the question，不用 *answer of the question。

come down to

The answer comes down to ... = 答案歸結為／取決於……，語意接近 depend on。

argue that / assume that / think that

寫作時可避免只用 think that，依語氣改用 argue that（主張）或 assume that（假設／認為）。

while + S + V

while they hit the books = 當他們用功讀書時。hit the books 是慣用語。

最容易考錯： effective（有效的） vs. efficient（有效率的）；effect（名詞影響／效果） vs. affect（動詞影響） vs. effort（努力）。

文章脈絡整理

說明：錄音中老師是逐句講解文章，但 ASR 未保留完整書面原文；因此這裡是依老師講解內容「重建」文章脈絡，不宣稱是課本逐字原文。
Should you listen to music while studying? This question has sparked debate. As a matter of fact, music can offer some advantages to studying. Studying can be stressful, so listening to music may help reduce stress and put you in a good mood. Research suggests that a good mood is linked to effective learning. Some kinds of music may also help students absorb and memorize new information. However, music can also be distracting. Loud or fast music, especially songs with lyrics, may get in the way of your thinking process and make your mind wander. Overall, the answer comes down to what kind of music you listen to. If music helps you study, choose lighter music, avoid distracting lyrics, and keep the volume low.

中文理解

你讀書時應該聽音樂嗎？ 這個問題引發了辯論。事實上，音樂對讀書可能有一些好處。讀書可能很有壓力，因此聽音樂或許能幫助減輕壓力，並讓你保持好心情。研究顯示，良好的心情與有效學習有關。有些類型的音樂也可能幫助學生吸收並記住新資訊。 然而，音樂也可能造成分心。吵雜或節奏很快的音樂，尤其是有歌詞的歌曲，可能妨礙你的思考過程，讓你走神。 總而言之，答案取決於你聽的是哪一種音樂。如果音樂確實能幫助你讀書，可以選擇較輕柔的音樂、避開容易分心的歌詞，並把音量保持低。

課堂摘要｜考前 5 分鐘版

  • 主題：讀書時聽音樂究竟幫助還是干擾學習。
  • debate / controversy：辯論、爭議。
  • 事實上：as a matter of fact / actually / in reality。
  • 音樂的可能好處：reduce stress、put you in a good mood；好心情與 effective learning 有關。
  • 研究顯示：research suggests / shows / indicates / demonstrates ...；寫作要學會同義替換。
  • 關聯：be linked to / be related to / be associated with / be connected with。
  • 核心易混：effect / affect / effort；effective / efficient。
  • 吸收新資訊：absorb and memorize new information。
  • 音樂的可能壞處：get in the way of your thinking process、interfere with、hinder、disrupt、disturb、impede；make your mind wander。
  • 結論：the answer comes down to / depends on what kind of music you listen to；避免歌詞、音量保持低。
  • volume：核心概念是「量」；可指音量、容量／體積、數量、冊；turn up/down the volume。
  • 寫作升級：think that → argue that / assume that。
  • 最後背誦句：Some people argue that music is actually a distraction while they hit the books.
