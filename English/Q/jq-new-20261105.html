先記這六個公式，這堂課就抓住大半核心。

感官動詞
see / hear / notice + O + 原 V / V-ing / p.p.
原 V：看見完整動作；V-ing：看見進行中；p.p.：受詞被動。
被動感官
be seen + to V / V-ing
改成被動，原形動詞前要補 to。
虛受詞
find / make + it + adj. / n. + to V
it 先占位置，真正的內容在後面。
未來式
will + 原 V
be going to：打算；be about to：馬上就要。
進行式
be + V-ing
現在、過去、未來某個時間點「正在」做。
現在完成式
have / has + p.p.
三大意思：經驗、持續、完成後與現在相關。

03｜必背片語 Phrases

paint the town red

出去狂歡、盡情玩樂

We painted the town red last night.

paint someone black

課堂用法：抹黑某人

The media painted the politician black.

補充：日常英文較常用 smear someone、portray someone in a bad light。

take ... for granted

視……為理所當然

Don't take your parents' love for granted.

不是 take ... of course。

make it a point to V

特別重視／刻意做到某事

I make it a point to practice every morning.

make it a habit to V

養成做某事的習慣

Make it a habit to read daily.

have been to / have gone to

曾去過（經驗）／已經去了（可能尚未回來）

I have been to Taipei five times.

04｜文法 Grammar

依老師上課順序整理，並將容易混淆的說法補充釐清。

句型寶典 p.31

使役動詞 have / make

have + 人 + 原 V
have + 物 + p.p.
make + 人 + 原 V

You'd better have someone fix the house as soon as possible.

You'd better have the house repaired as soon as possible.

Crayon Shin-chan made the room messy again.

逐字稿使用「made the room messed up」。較自然可寫 messed up the room again 或 made the room messy again。
p.32–34

感官動詞

see / watch / hear / notice / feel / observe + O + 原 V / V-ing / p.p.

I saw him mop the floor and do the dishes.

I noticed my mom playing a game.

She saw her brother's friend sent to the hospital.

原 V = 完整動作；V-ing = 動作進行中；p.p. = 被動。並非只能用原 V 或 V-ing。
p.34

感官動詞的被動

He was seen to take the money.
He was seen taking the money.

主動：They saw him take the money.

被動：He was seen to take the money.

原 V 變被動時要加 to；V-ing 通常保留 V-ing。
p.25

狀態／結果動詞

keep / leave / get / make + O + 補語

keep：維持狀態；leave：留下、放任某狀態；get：使變成；make：造成某結果。

She kept the door open.
He left me waiting.
The news made me sad.

make 不一定「故意」，leave 也不一定「非故意」；要依句意判斷。
p.26–27

虛受詞 it

find it + adj. + to V
make it possible (for 人) to V
make it a rule / point / habit to V

I find it intriguing to teach English.

Technology makes it possible for us to learn online.

it 是「占位符」，真正受詞是後面的 to V 或子句。
文法寶典 p.23

未來式的多種說法

will + 原 V
be going to + 原 V
be about to + 原 V

will：未來預測、意願等。
going to：打算／有跡象將發生。
about to：即將、馬上。
現在簡單式：固定時刻表。
現在進行式：已安排的未來活動。

shall 不限只能第一人稱，但現代英文多用於 I / we 的提議、詢問（Shall we...?）。未來用法不應以 99% 等固定機率判斷。
進行式

三種進行式

am/is/are + V-ing
was/were + V-ing
will be + V-ing

JQ is playing now.
At 8 yesterday, I was taking a class.
At 10 tomorrow, I will be studying.

常見搭配：When he came, the baby was sleeping.

know、believe、own 等狀態動詞通常不用進行式，但有例外；進行式也可表暫時性、變化過程。
完成式 ⭐

現在完成式

have / has + p.p.

經驗：I have been to Taipei five times.
持續：I have lived here for ten years.
完成／結果：I have finished my homework.

常見訊號：just, already, yet, ever, never, so far, since, for。

口訣：過去開始，現在有關。
時態比較

過去式 vs. 現在完成式

I went to Taipei last year.
去年去台北（明確過去時間）。

I have been to Taipei five times.
截至現在累積五次經驗。

I had finished my homework before he arrived.
他到達前，我就已完成（過去完成式）。

現在完成式不只表「人生經驗」，也能表示持續與現在結果；過去式也可以描述個人經驗。
