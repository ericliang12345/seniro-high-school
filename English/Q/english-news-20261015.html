03 / 核心單字、音標與一字多義

用「意思 + 搭配詞 + 例句」記憶，而不是只背單字表。

🔊 使用裝置內建英文語音；在離線環境需你的裝置已安裝英文語音。IPA 為常見美式讀法近似標記。

⚠️ 一字多義重點：passage「段落／通道」；bag「袋子／奪得」；heat「熱／預賽」；form「表格／競技狀態」；secure「安全的／確保、取得」；leave「離開／讓某人處於⋯狀態」。
單字在句子中的意思，要靠前後文與搭配詞判斷。

05 / 段考與作文必考文法

每個句型都搭配講義原文或老師課堂中的例句。

① because 與 because of

because + 主詞 + 動詞（完整子句）
because of + 名詞 / V-ing

✓ I feel nervous because I care about my grades.

✓ I feel nervous because of the exam.

✗ because the exam（只有名詞，缺完整句子）

② about 是介系詞

worry about + 名詞 / V-ing
be worried about + 名詞 / V-ing

✓ I worry about forgetting the words I have studied.

✗ I worry about forget the words.

句中的 that 可省略：the words (that) I have studied。

③ despite 與 although

despite / in spite of + 名詞 / V-ing
although + 主詞 + 動詞

✓ Despite the injury, he won gold.

✓ Although he was injured, he won gold.

✗ Despite he was injured,...

④ diagnose 診斷句型

diagnose + 人 + with + 疾病
be diagnosed with + 疾病

✓ A doctor diagnosed him with acute tendon inflammation.

✓ He was diagnosed with inflammation.

疾病名稱 inflammation 是名詞；acute 是形容詞。

⑤ leave + 受詞 + 補語

leave + 人 + 形容詞 / P.P.

✓ The injury left him worried.

✓ The result left me disappointed.

這裡的 leave 不是「離開」，是「讓某人處在某狀態」。

⑥ remind 與 give it one’s all

remind + 人 + to + 原形動詞
give it + my / your / his / her + all

✓ He reminded himself to stay relaxed.

✓ I will give it my all.

one’s 必須隨主詞改變：I → my、he → his。

⑦ 兩種「晉級」的介系詞

advance from + 起點
advance to + 下一輪

✓ He advanced from the heats.

✓ He advanced to the final.

同一句可以併用：He advanced from the heats to the final.

⑧ 新聞標題與報導時態

標題：Wang bags gold.
本文：Wang won gold.

新聞標題常用現在式來呈現已發生的事，內文多使用過去式；轉述更早的經歷可用過去完成式。

✓ He said he had been under pressure.

⑨ 關係子句與分詞構句

the words (that) I have studied
..., adding that + 子句

that 作受詞可以省略：I studied the words → the words (that) I studied。

adding that 接後續說明，是新聞常見的補充寫法。

⑩ 說壓力與有共同點

be / feel under pressure
have something / nothing / a lot in common

✓ What puts you under pressure before an exam?

✓ We have a lot in common.

What 在這題是主詞，單數問法用 puts；不是 what put you...。

06 / 口說與作文輸出練習

用簡單但正確的句子，逐漸把一個片語變成整段短文。

SPEAKING Q1

What do swimmers and students have in common?

游泳選手和學生有什麼共同點？

We both need to practice and stay focused on our goals.
Both of us face pressure, make mistakes and learn from failure.
我們都需要練習、專注目標，也會承受壓力、犯錯並從失敗學習。
SPEAKING Q2

What puts you under pressure before an exam?

考試前有什麼會讓你感到壓力？

I feel nervous because I care a lot about my grades.
I worry about forgetting the words that I have studied.
我緊張，因為我很在意成績；也擔心忘記已經讀過的單字。
FOREST WRITING METHOD · 句型填空

寫一段 80–120 字的英文短文：How do you deal with pressure?

依照老師的「固定句型開頭 + 自己填內容」方法；依序回答壓力來源、面對方式、收穫。

Step 1 · 感受

I feel nervous because ...

I am worried about V-ing / 名詞 ...

Step 2 · 做法

To deal with pressure, I ...

I remind myself to ...

Step 3 · 心態

Despite the difficulties, I ...

Step 4 · 結尾

I will keep practicing and give it my all.

0 words
示範：
Before an exam, I often feel nervous because I care about my grades. Sometimes I worry about forgetting the words that I have studied. To deal with pressure, I make a study plan and practice every day. I also remind myself to stay relaxed and take a deep breath. Despite the difficulties, I try to maintain my own pace. I believe that mistakes are part of the learning process. If I keep practicing, I can build confidence and become more resilient. Next time, I will focus on my own lane and give it my all.

09 / 講義延伸 · 加分文法與題目

講義第 15–23 頁還有延伸練習，雖然本段上課錄音未逐題講授，也可用來補強下一次段考。

授與動詞：S + V + 人 + 物 ／ to、for、of 差別

常見規則：give / send / show / teach + 物 + to + 人；buy / make / write / sing + 物 + for + 人；ask a question of somebody 為較正式用法。

He paid me nine hundred dollars. = He paid nine hundred dollars to me.
I bought JQ a birthday present. = I bought a birthday present for JQ.
May I ask you a question? = May I ask a question of you?

⚠️ 注意各動詞固定搭配，不是所有雙受詞都能隨意互換介系詞。

片語雙字動詞：10 組段考很愛考的搭配詞
turn down — 拒絕 / 調低
go over — 複習
write down — 記下
look after — 照顧
put off — 延期
give up — 放棄
run out of — 用完
look forward to + V-ing — 期待
work out — 解決；運動
find out — 查明

講義例句：We've run out of paper.（紙用完了）；I look forward to meeting you.（期待見面，to 後接 V-ing）。

身體部位前的介系詞：by / on / in / across

基本公式：V + 人 + 介系詞 + the + 身體部位。

by the hand / sleeve
抓住某人手／袖子
on the shoulder / head
輕拍肩膀／擊打頭部表面
in the stomach
朝腹部打一拳
across the face
甩一巴掌掠過臉部

講義例句：He patted me on the shoulder.（他拍我的肩膀。）注意此句用 the；He patted my shoulder 也正確，但句型不同。
