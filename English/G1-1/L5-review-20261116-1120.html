Lesson 5｜The Life of a Plastic Bag

塑膠袋的一生｜一頁式段考重點筆記

🔊 每個英文單字、片語與句子旁可點選朗讀（0.8 倍速）

段考故事文法片語單字新增精讀測驗

段考範圍

課本 L4–L6：課文、例句、Exercise、單字片語；字彙書 Unit 1–3；雜誌三篇閱讀。先熟讀課本例句與習作。

課文四段故事

① 塑膠袋從工廠送到便利商店，隨孩子去海灘，卻被遺棄。② 在海上漂流，被大魚吞下；魚因吞食塑膠而死。③ 魚分解後，塑膠袋被沖上岸，學童淨灘。④ 塑膠污染進入食物鏈，主角期待被妥善回收。

文法① 使役動詞

make / have / let + 受詞 + 原形動詞；例：They let me stay there alone.（他們讓我獨自留下。）

文法② 表原因

because + 主詞＋動詞；because of / due to / owing to + 名詞或 V-ing。例：Because the weather was bad, we stayed home. / Because of the bad weather, we stayed home. 注意：be due to 常見於句中，但 due to 不限只能接在 be 後面。

文法③ 被動語態

be + 過去分詞：I was sent from the factory to a convenience store. / We were swallowed by a huge fish.

文法④ 關係代名詞

the birds up above, who were flying so high and free：逗號＋who 補充已明確的鳥群；限定用法通常不加逗號。

文法⑤ 狀態變化

be getting + 形容詞：It was getting dark. / The condition is getting worse. 比較級＋and＋比較級：more and more / fatter and fatter。

文法⑥ 其他句型

had better (not) + 原形動詞；not that + 形容詞：We weren't that nutritious.；end up + V-ing / 地點片語；It occurred to me that ...

高頻片語

from A to B（從 A 到 B）；for a while（一陣子）；come along（出現）；take ... along（帶著）；on my own / by myself（獨自）；all of a sudden（突然）；be surrounded by（被包圍）；follow the currents（隨波逐流）；be crowded with（擠滿）；because of（因為）；according to（根據）；be tired of（厭倦）。

必背單字

plastic 塑膠；convenience 便利；eagerly 熱切地；surround 包圍；drift 漂流；envy 羨慕；crowded 擁擠的；obviously 顯然；swallow 吞下；nutritious 有營養的；decompose 分解；shore 岸邊；tongs 夾子；littering 亂丟垃圾；consume 消耗；poisonous 有毒的；substance 物質；recycling 回收；harm 傷害；properly 妥善地。

📌 最新課堂補充｜第三、四段精讀

依老師補充摘要新增，英文逐句可按 🔊 以 0.8 倍速朗讀。

📖 第二段回顧｜漂流、羨慕、被魚吞下

作者用第一人稱 I 和擬人法，讓塑膠袋有孤單、羨慕等情緒，帶出環境污染問題。

• I was surrounded by plastic cups, plates, and straws.

• After days of drifting, I started to envy the birds up above, who were flying so high and free.

• We obviously weren't that nutritious.

• the fish died after a few days.

🌊 第三段｜分解 → 上岸 → 淨灘 → 食物鏈

魚屍分解，塑膠袋回到海洋；大浪把它沖上岸，學童拿著袋子和夾子撿垃圾。塑膠被海洋生物吃下，毒素可能經海鮮進入人體。老師以連鎖效應說明污染後果。

• The fish's body slowly decomposed.

• A big wave came and threw me up onto the shore.

• Some schoolchildren walked up to me.

• They were carrying bags and pairs of tongs.

• Because of all the littering, a lot of plastic products end up in the ocean.

• The poisonous substances from the plastic end up inside us and make us feel sick.

♻️ 第四段｜反思與希望

人類製造塑膠讓生活更便利，卻因不當使用反而危害環境。塑膠袋希望下一位主人妥善使用它。

• According to a plastic bottle nearby, the humans are going to take us to a recycling plant.

• They produce us to make their lives better, but then they misuse us and make everyone's life worse!

• I'm tired of floating around, killing fish, and causing harm to the environment.

• I really hope that my future owner will use me properly in my next life.

⭐ 必考 make：兩種結構

① make + 受詞 + 原形動詞：讓某人做某事。② make + 受詞 + 形容詞／名詞（受詞補語）：使某人變得……或成為……。考題先看 make 後面受詞後接的是動詞原形，還是形容詞／名詞。

• make us feel sick

• make their lives better

• make everyone's life worse

• Your encouragement has made me a better man.

⭐ hope 與 wish｜快速區分

hope：對可能實現的事抱持希望，常接 that 子句。wish：可表與現實不符的願望（現在相反用過去式、過去相反用過去完成式），也能用於祝福。注意：hope 不代表事情一定很可能實現；wish 也可表一般祝願。

• I hope that my future owner will use me properly.

• I wish I had more time.

• I wish I had studied harder.

• I wish you a Merry Christmas.

⭐ because / because of｜選擇題秒判

because + 完整子句（S + V）；because of + 名詞、名詞片語或 V-ing。看到 littering 是動名詞，選 because of。

• Because of all the littering, a lot of plastic products end up in the ocean.

• Because people litter, plastic products end up in the ocean.

🧠 字根與易混淆字

decompose = de-（分離）+ compose（組成）→ 分解；misuse = mis-（錯誤）+ use → 誤用。environment：環境；surroundings：周遭環境。according to：根據某人／消息；based on：以……為基礎。nearby 可作形容詞或副詞。trash 與 litter 用法不同，兩者都可見於英美英文；「用字切換象徵飄洋過海」是課堂的文學解讀，不是語言學上的必然結論。

• decompose

• misuse

• environment

• surroundings

• according to

• based on

• nearby

• trash

• litter

🗣️ 高頻片語｜會翻譯也要會造句

throw up：嘔吐；throw + 受詞 + up：把……往上拋。walk up to：走向。end up + 地點／V-ing：最後落得……。be tired of + N／V-ing：厭倦。cause harm to / do harm to：傷害。

• throw up

• throw me up onto the shore

• walk up to me

• end up in the ocean

• be tired of floating around

• cause harm to the environment
